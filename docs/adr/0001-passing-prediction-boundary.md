# Passing prediction terminology

Status: accepted for prototype implementation

The user requests prediction as a product capability. Candidate passes retain their
existing meaning. "Passing prediction" refers to estimated travel, interception risk
and receiver-control feasibility under the explicit heuristic assumptions.

The current difficulty and ranking scores are not completion probabilities.
No real-world accuracy claim is supported by synthetic or regression tests. The app
separates observed synthetic outcomes, alternative candidate estimates, and viewer choice.
Generative AI selects verified facts through evidence tools rather than manufacturing
numerical predictions. Human football review remains a separate validation step.
