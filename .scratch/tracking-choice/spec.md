# Tracking receiver-choice experiment

Predeclared before training. Data: original Metrica sample-data commit e706dd506b360d69d9d123d5b8026e7294b13996, CSV games 1 and 2. Official definitions make PASS a completed same-team possession transfer. BALL LOST/BALL OUT lack reliable intended receiver. Therefore target is receiver choice conditional on a completed PASS, never completion probability or best-pass quality.

Use only start-frame tracking coordinates, passer identity for lookup, and candidate teammates. Receiver event To supplies the label only. No event endpoints, end frame/time, future tracking, shirt identities as features, or outcome-based negative labels. Require all 11 players per side at release, valid on-pitch positions, chosen receiver in candidates. Exclude HEAD/CROSS/CLEARANCE/DEEP BALL subtypes to focus on ordinary/through ground-pass-like choices, while acknowledging pass height is not exhaustively annotated.

Fixed models (no tuning): CatBoostRanker PairLogit depth 4, 300 iterations, learning rate .03, seed 42; logistic candidate-choice baseline with scaled identical inputs; nearest-teammate baseline. Primary train game 1/test game 2. Reverse train game 2/test game 1 as a separate robustness fold; no shared fitted weights or tuning. Features: distance, absolute x/y displacement, candidate and passer distance to touchline/endline, nearest defender at passer/receiver, minimum defender-to-segment distance and defender count within 3m of segment. No attack direction inferred from future data.

Report top-1, top-3, MRR, sample counts/exclusions, fixed 5-minute-block bootstrap interval for top-1 difference (not a match-generalization CI). Exploratory gate: top-1 improves at least 5 percentage points over logistic and nearest teammate in BOTH folds. Regardless of gate, no production promotion: only two matches, successful-pass selection bias, no actual failure-target supervision, no validated screenshot-domain mapping, and limited published use terms. Record source hashes, save ignored artifacts/predictions, add leakage and reproduction tests. Do not tune after inspecting test results.
